# Vision

The AI Assistant acts as a business advisor, helping shop owners understand their business and make better decisions.

It is not a chatbot.

It is an intelligent business assistant.

---

# Goals

- Analyze sales
- Analyze profits
- Analyze expenses
- Detect unusual business behavior
- Predict stock shortages
- Recommend actions

---

# Data Sources

The AI Assistant analyzes:

- Products
- Sales
- Purchases
- Expenses
- Inventory
- Customers
- Suppliers

---

# Smart Insights

Examples:

Today's sales increased by 12%.
